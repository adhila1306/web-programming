ICET

Ilahia College of Engineering and Technology (ICET) is a private, co-educational minority institution located in Mulavoor, Muvattupuzha, Kerala. Established in 2002 under the Ilahia Trust, it stands as one of the pioneering self-financing engineering colleges in the state. The institution is approved by the AICTE and affiliated with the APJ Abdul Kalam Kerala Technological University (KTU). ICET offers a variety of undergraduate (B.Tech) and postgraduate (M.Tech, MBA, MCA) programs spanning traditional and emerging technological fields. Committed to social responsibility, the campus provides structured scholarships and supportive educational schemes while maintaining active placement and training cells linked with major corporate recruiters.
